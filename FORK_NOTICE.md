# Fork and Modification Notice

Notch Master is a modified version of [Boring Notch](https://github.com/TheBoredTeam/boring.notch).

## Upstream work

The original Boring Notch source, project history, authorship, copyright notices, GNU GPL v3 license, and third-party notices are retained. Notch Master does not claim ownership of upstream work.

## Fork modifications

The Notch Master fork is maintained by [PWL31](https://github.com/PWL31). The first public fork release was prepared on 2026-07-24.

Material modifications include:

- Notch Master product branding and fork-specific release metadata.
- Codex weekly usage display backed by the locally installed Codex client.
- Used/Remaining display modes, reset countdown, and remaining-capacity status thresholds.
- Removal of the persistent menu bar icon.
- External-display invisible closed-notch mode with hover and gesture activation.
- Configurable Quick Folder shortcuts with automatic and custom icons.
- Removal of the upstream Sparkle update feed to prevent cross-project updates.

All fork modifications are released under GNU GPL v3. See the Git history for file-level authorship and dates.
